{% header "h2", "Functional Colors" %}

In addition to the primary brand color, the design system includes a set of functional colors for communicating status and providing feedback to the user.

  • Red: Used for error states, destructive actions, and critical alerts.
  • Amber (Yellow): Used for warnings and other non-critical alerts.
  • Green: Used for success states and positive feedback.
